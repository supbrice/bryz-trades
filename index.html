What the statuses mean
  • Open — not realized. Still in the journal. Excluded from realized profit and loss.
  • Closed — recorded exit. Uses the exit price, fees, and exit date you entered. Bryz Trades does not check the market.
  • Cancelled — not realized. Excluded from realized profit and loss.

How saving works

The journal is stored in this browser’s local storage on this device. It is not an account, and it is not sent to a server.

Another browser, another device, or a private-browsing window has its own empty copy. Private browsing may delete the journal when the window closes.

Clearing site data, cookies, or history for this site deletes the journal. Export a JSON backup first if the records matter.

A failed save or a failed import leaves the previous copy in place. Before an upgrade or import, Bryz Trades also keeps a backup copy in this browser.

Trade

Add trade

Profit and loss uses entry price when both prices are filled. If entry price is blank, average cost is used. Long return divides by shares × that price. Short return divides by the sale proceeds, shares × that price, not by margin.

The closing price is the exit you write down when the whole position is closed in one lot. Choosing a target or stop reason does not mean the market reached that level. Chart prices are not used.

Partial exits

Average-cost method. Every exit uses the same recorded entry price. Selling some shares leaves the rest open. Each lot has its own fees. A blank fee on a lot is treated as $0.00.

Unrealized P/L is N/A until you enter a mark. The mark is manual. It is not read from the chart.

Levels checklist

Optional, and only a comparison with a close you paste. Hard stop is checked before get-out. A close equal to a level is not under it. This does not place or cancel orders.

Checklist

Manual entry

A hand-typed day result from before trade-level tracking, or an account note you want to keep. It is labeled manual and is not a closed trade.

Split the manual entry

This split only describes the manual amount. It is not added to realized P/L.

Account adjustment

A deposit, withdrawal, or other account change. It is not trading profit or loss and is not added to either total.
